Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026. Provided by PAG Apps.

Ballin' is a basketball stat-tracking app for parents, coaches and volunteer scorekeepers. This policy explains what the app stores, where it is stored, and what leaves your phone. The short version: your games stay on your phone.

What the app stores

When you use Ballin', you may enter:

  • team names, and the name of the league or location of a game
  • player names (optional), jersey numbers and positions
  • player photos and team logos, if you choose to add them
  • the plays you record during a game, such as shots, rebounds and fouls

All of this is stored on your device, in the app's own storage. We do not receive it, and we cannot see it.

Players are often children. Entering their names and photos is your choice, and that information stays on your device unless you choose to share it (see below).

What leaves your phone

Only these things, and only in these ways:

  • Backups you make. If you use Backup & Restore, the app creates a file with your games, teams and leagues and opens your phone's share sheet. Where the file goes (email, Files, a cloud drive) is your choice. Backups never include player photos or team logos.
  • Exports you make. Box scores and play-by-play exports go wherever you choose to share them.
  • Sync, if you turn it on. Sync is off unless you enable it in the app's settings and enter a server address. If you do, the app sends your games, teams and rosters to that server. Photos and logos are never sent.
  • Update checks. The app checks for updates to itself through Expo, the service that delivers them. These checks send technical information only (app version, platform and update channel), not anything you have entered.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No selling or sharing of your information

Testing through TestFlight

If you are testing Ballin' through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect crash reports, usage data and any feedback you send, under Apple's own privacy policy. We may see crash reports and feedback that Apple passes on to us.

Deleting your information

Everything is on your device. Delete a game, team or league in the app to remove it, or delete the app to remove everything. If you made backup files or exports, delete them wherever you saved them.

Children

Ballin' is meant for adults keeping stats, not for children to use directly. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children, because the information you enter stays on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear at this address with a new effective date.
